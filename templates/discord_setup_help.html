Fix Discord OAuth Error

Error: "invalid OAuth2 redirect_URL"

This happens when the redirect URI in your Discord application doesn't match what your app is sending.

🔧 How to Fix This:
Step 1: Open Discord Developer Portal

Click this link to go directly to your Discord application:

Open Discord Developer Portal
Step 2: Add Redirect URIs

Scroll down to "Redirects" section and add these URIs:

📋 Redirect URIs to Add:
http://localhost:10000/auth/discord/callback
https://localhost:10000/auth/discord/callback
http://127.0.0.1:10000/auth/discord/callback
https://127.0.0.1:10000/auth/discord/callback
https://recenttablist.onrender.com/auth/discord/callback
http://recenttablist.onrender.com/auth/discord/callback
Copy each URI exactly as shown above and paste them one by one into the Discord Developer Portal.
🎯 After Adding the URIs:
  1. Click "Save Changes" in Discord Developer Portal
  2. Come back to your profile
  3. Try clicking "Connect" under Discord Account again
  4. It should work now! 🎉
