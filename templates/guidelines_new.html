📖 Official Guidelines

Everything you need to know about submitting to RTL

🎯 Level Submission Requirements

📈 Recent Tab Requirement
Levels must be found in the recent tab. There is no specific time duration for how recent they must be.
💾 Level Backup
Make copies of verified levels in case a list level is suddenly deleted from the servers.
🎮 Difficulty Assessment
When submitting, provide your difficulty opinion in reference to the list and the in-game star rating system (e.g., "I think X is top 60 and a 6 star").

🚫 Level Restrictions

❌ Not Allowed

Nerfed/Buffed Levels
Pre-existing rated levels that have been modified
Stolen Levels
Levels that copy pre-existing rated levels
Own Levels
Cannot be your own level made for the list
Auto Levels
Auto levels will not be placed on the list
Platformers
Platformer levels will not be placed

⚠️ Special Cases

Partial Copying
Somewhat allowed, but stolen sections must not resemble the original
Human Input Required
Must require at least 1 human-made input to be beaten
Secret Ways
You may fix secret ways with mod approval
Coins
Collecting coins is not required for placement
Filters
You may use in-game filters to filter your recent tab

✅ Verification Requirements

Required Elements

🛡️ Cheat Indicator
Must have MegaHack cheat indicator enabled (or any mod with cheat indicator)
🔊 Audible Clicks
You must have audible clicks in your verification
🎬 Full Completion
Show the complete level from start to finish
📹 Clear Quality
Video must be clear enough to verify legitimacy
Vanilla Players: If you play vanilla Geometry Dash, the cheat indicator rule is an exception for you.

Additional Notes

📸 Handcam
Not required but recommended
🎥 Platform
YouTube links preferred for verification videos
🔊 Audio
Game audio must be audible throughout
✂️ Editing
Minimal editing allowed, no cuts during gameplay

🤝 Community Standards

✅ Expected Behavior

• Treat all members with respect
• Be honest about achievements
• Show good sportsmanship
• Provide constructive feedback
• Help newcomers
• Follow all rules
• Report issues to staff
• Stay updated on rule changes

❌ Prohibited Behavior

• Harassment or bullying
• Hate speech or discrimination
• Spam or excessive self-promotion
• Doxxing or sharing personal info
• Impersonation
• Creating unnecessary drama
• Inappropriate content
• Ban evasion
